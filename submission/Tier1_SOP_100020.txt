Meridian SOC — Tier 1 SOP for rule 100020

On every 100020 alert, search the next 120 seconds for a successful SSH
login (Wazuh rule 5715) for the same source IP and the same username.

- If success is present and the account is a known staff or service
  identity: close as VPN or job retry. Do not escalate.
- If there is no success, or the account is unused, root, or unknown:
  escalate to Tier 2.
